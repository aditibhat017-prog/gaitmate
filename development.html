ENGINEERING PROCESS

Development

GaitMate is an evolving biomedical engineering project. Development focuses on translating the initial concept into a testable wearable sensing platform through iterative design, prototyping, and evaluation.

Development Roadmap

01 — Concept

Define the clinical problem, target anatomy, proposed measurements, and overall wearable system.

02 — Design

Develop the sensor architecture, placement strategy, digital platform, and analysis framework.

03 — Prototype

Translate the proposed system into physical sensor prototypes and an integrated wearable format.

04 — Bench Testing

Characterize sensor response, repeatability, durability, and measurement behavior under controlled conditions.

05 — Validation

Future studies would evaluate whether measurements correspond to meaningful biomechanical and rehabilitation outcomes.

CURRENT WORK

Prototype Development

Current development is focused on translating the proposed wearable architecture into a physical prototype. This includes investigating sensing approaches, wearable materials, attachment methods, electronics, and methods for collecting and interpreting sensor measurements.

Design Iteration

GaitMate follows an iterative engineering process. Design decisions are expected to evolve as prototypes are built and evaluated. Future versions of this page will document major design iterations, experimental results, and lessons learned throughout development.

Testing Strategy

Sensor Response

Evaluate how the sensing element responds across controlled mechanical conditions.

Repeatability

Determine whether repeated measurements under similar conditions produce consistent responses.

Wearability

Examine attachment, flexibility, comfort, movement interference, and durability of the wearable format.

Limitations

GaitMate remains an early-stage research and engineering project. The proposed sensing system has not yet been clinically validated, and future experimental work will be required to determine the accuracy, reliability, and clinical relevance of its measurements.

What's Next?

Upcoming development will focus on sensor prototyping, benchtop characterization, wearable integration, and continued development of the data-analysis and digital-feedback system.
