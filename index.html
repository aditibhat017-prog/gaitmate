GaitMate

Wearable biosensing for smarter rehabilitation.

GaitMate is a wearable biosensing platform exploring how localized strain monitoring and digital feedback could support ankle sprain rehabilitation.

The system combines wearable sensor patches with data analysis and a digital interface designed to help patients and clinicians better understand movement throughout recovery.

Explore the Technology
THE PROBLEM

Recovery happens outside the clinic.

Ankle sprains are common musculoskeletal injuries, but much of rehabilitation takes place away from direct clinical observation. GaitMate explores whether wearable sensing could provide additional information about movement throughout recovery.
