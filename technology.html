Technology

GaitMate combines wearable sensing, localized movement measurements, and digital feedback to explore a new approach to ankle sprain rehabilitation.

How GaitMate Works

01 — Sense

Wearable sensor patches collect localized mechanical measurements from key regions around the ankle.

02 — Analyze

Sensor measurements are processed to characterize movement patterns and changes during rehabilitation.

03 — Understand

Data is translated into accessible feedback for patients and longitudinal information for clinicians.

Wearable Sensor System

GaitMate's proposed wearable system uses a network of sensor patches positioned around the ankle to capture localized mechanical changes during movement.

Patch Architecture

Each patch is being designed as a layered wearable consisting of a skin-safe adhesive layer, sensing component, and protective outer layer.

Digital Platform

The proposed GaitMate digital platform is designed to transform sensor measurements into understandable rehabilitation feedback while allowing clinicians to examine changes over time.
